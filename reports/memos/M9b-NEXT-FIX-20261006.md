## The one fix

**Retune effective tau at decode on `20261006-021245`.**

Trained with `tau_train=1.0`. Adding `d * log(pi)` to **raw subclass logits before softmax** gives

`tau_eff = 1.0 - d`.

| `d` (`--subclass-decode-logit-adjust`) | `tau_eff` | intent |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 (default, shipped) | 1.0 | current eval (contract 0.162) |
| 0.5 | 0.5 | half-undo rare-class boost |
| 1.0 | 0.0 | training CE decision boundary (closest to posterior / majority) |

Sweep **d ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0}**. Pick the smallest d that gets **contract ≥ 0.20** with **correspondence still ≥ 0.25** (and do not care about merger for #112).
